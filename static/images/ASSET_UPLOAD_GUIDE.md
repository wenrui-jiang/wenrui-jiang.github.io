## Asset Upload Guide

- Thesis model diagrams: put files in `static/images/research-models/`
- Certificates (CET-4/6 etc.): put files in `static/images/certificates/`
- Honors/Awards images: put files in `static/images/honors/`

Recommended filename format:

- `thesis-model-01.png`
- `cet4-certificate.jpg`
- `cet6-certificate.jpg`
- `scholarship-2024-09.jpg`
- `mcm-icm-honorable-mention-2022.jpg`
